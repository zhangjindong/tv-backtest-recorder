## Detailed description (max 16000 chars)

```
The most annoying part of backtesting isn't the trading — it's the bookkeeping.

Draw a position, take a screenshot, switch to Excel, type a row, paste the image…
Do that a few dozen times a day and you stop journaling altogether.

TV Backtest Recorder turns that whole loop into one click.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

■ HOW IT WORKS

1. On a TradingView chart, draw your trade with the Long Position / Short
   Position tool (entry, stop and target).
2. A floating panel appears and automatically lists every position tool on the
   chart, with entry / stop / target price, risk, reward and risk-reward ratio.
3. Click "复制这一笔（含截图）" (Copy this trade + screenshot) — a full-screen
   capture plus all the numbers land in your clipboard at once.
4. Switch to Excel / WPS and hit Ctrl+V on the next empty row: the numbers fill
   the columns and the screenshot comes along. Repeat for the next trade.

Forgot to copy a few? Click "复制全部（含截图）" to export every position tool on
the chart at once — multiple rows, each with its own image.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

■ OR EXPORT A REAL .XLSX

Click "导出 Excel" and the extension downloads an .xlsx directly:

· Fixed header: time · symbol · timeframe · side · entry · stop · target ·
  risk · reward · R:R · screenshot · note · P&L · win rate
  (the last two are left blank for you to fill in)
· The screenshot is written as an *embedded cell image* (WPS DISPIMG format) —
  it lives inside the cell instead of floating over the sheet
· Every cell is horizontally and vertically centered, ready to use
· Duplicate images are stored once, so the file stays small
· Generated locally on your machine — no server involved

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

■ WHAT THE PANEL SHOWS

· Draggable panel — grab the title bar and put it anywhere; position is remembered
· Long trades in red, short trades in green
· "✓ 已校验" — the computed stop price matches the chart's raw anchor
· "仅公式" — derived from the formula only; double-check it visually
· Copied rows turn green and are marked "✓ 已复制", with a progress counter at
  the bottom
· Hidden drawing tools on the chart are ignored automatically
· The dot in the corner is a health light: green = tools found, red = read
  failure. Click it to copy a diagnostic report.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

■ PRIVACY

· No network access — the extension never sends data anywhere. No analytics,
  no tracking, no ads.
· Nothing is uploaded — your prices and screenshots stay on your own machine.
· Local storage only — panel position and the session's records live in
  chrome.storage.local.
· Permissions — Chrome requires the "read data on all websites" permission for
  the screenshot API (captureVisibleTab) to work. The content script itself only
  ever runs on tradingview.com.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

■ KNOWN LIMITATIONS

· The screenshot covers the currently visible chart viewport. If the chart is
  zoomed in, press TradingView's "fit content" first.
· The multi-row export shares one screenshot — trades drawn on the same screen
  are literally the same picture.
· It reads TradingView's web chart API. A major TV redesign may require an
  update (the panel shows a red dot instead of failing silently).
· Tested with WPS Spreadsheets; Microsoft Excel has not been tested.
· This is a journaling utility only. It gives no signals, advice or predictions.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

■ QUICK START

1. Install, then pin the extension to the toolbar.
2. Open a TradingView chart page.
3. Draw a Long Position / Short Position tool.
4. Click "Copy this trade + screenshot".
5. Paste into Excel, or click "Export Excel".
```
